HOW TO ADD PHOTOS
==================

Drop a JPG photo into this "photos" folder, named after the person's id.
The site picks it up automatically - no code changes needed.
Example: a photo of Daniel Sadler must be saved as   photos/daniel.jpg
Square-ish photos look best (they are shown as circles).

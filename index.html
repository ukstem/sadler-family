A living family archive

One family. Three continents. Seven recorded generations.

In 1846, a young couple left Rajasthan, India, and crossed two oceans to the parish of Hanover, Jamaica. Their son Daniel — surveyor, philosopher, astrologer, teacher — married Elizabeth Malcolm of Castle Heights, and together they raised twelve children. This is their family, traced as far as the records and memories reach.

Now woven from two records

This archive merges the master family database (begun 2013–16), the family document of 2026, and oral-history notes taken in conversation with an older family member. Where the sources disagree — and they do — nothing is silently corrected: every conflict is flagged on the person’s record and gathered on the Data review page for the family to settle together. Names marked with * carry an inferred surname; dashed tree cards are people shown twice; the ⛑ flag marks records with an open question.
